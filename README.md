# WELL-CONNECTED

WELL-CONNECTED Mississippi Community Health Portal

## Core Platform Areas

- Community resources
- Population health research translation
- Policy tracking
- Mississippi county routing
- Community discussion topics
- Research requests
- Verified announcements
- Event and recruitment notices

## Verified Announcement Categories

The platform supports verified announcements for:

- Conferences
- Open houses
- Blood drives
- Recruitment events
- Research participation opportunities
- Prenatal and maternal health services
- Community alerts
- Time-sensitive notices
- Public health education highlights
